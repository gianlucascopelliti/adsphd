\chapter{Curriculum Vitae}\label{ch:curriculum}

Gianluca Scopelliti was born on 5th of September 1993 in Parma, Italy. From 2015
to 2020 he studied Computer Engineering at Politecnico di Torino, where he
obtained first the bachelor's degree in 2018 and then the master's degree in
2020, both degrees obtained with the maximum grade (110 with Laude). During the
academic year 2019-2020, he spent an exchange year in Belgium at KU Leuven, part
of the Erasmus+ mobility program. There, he pursued his master's thesis with the
DistriNet group with title ``Securing Smart Environments with Authentic
Execution'', under the supervision of Frank Piessens, Jan Tobias M\"{u}hlberg
and Fritz Alder. After completing his master's degree, he started his Ph.D. with
the MSCA ITN EID project 5GhOSTS, a collaboration between Ericsson Research
(Sweden) and KU Leuven. Thus, between 2020 and 2024 he spent approximately half
the time at Ericsson premises and half the time with the DistriNet group at KU
Leuven.
